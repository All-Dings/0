## Story

In [Bct-2093](1000001.md), a [Spaceship](200600001.md) called [Prometheus](200600001.md)
travels from [Earth](10500005.md) to a distant [Planet](10500004.md) in [Search](600091.md) of
the [Origin](60090.md) of [Human](40000001.md)-[Life](40000081.md).
The [Crew](180000006.md), including [Dr.-Elizabeth-Shaw](60020005.md) and
[Meredith_Vickers](60020005.md), follows a [Star-Map](700001.md) left by an
ancient [Civilization](180000000.md).  Upon [Arrival](600085.md), the [Exploration](600091.md)
of a mysterious [Structure](60100002.md) reveals [Traces](600168.md) of a powerful
and dangerous [Substance](60003.md). The [Android](200400004.md) [David-8](60020008.md)
studies the [Substance](60003.md) and secretly interacts with it, triggering unexpected and
deadly [Events](600085.md). As the [Situation](60069.md) escalates,
the [Crew](600063.md) faces the [Consequences](60091.md) of their [Discovery](60003.md),
leading to a [Struggle](600029.md) for [Survival](60003.md) and a deeper [Understanding](60187.md) of
the [Engineers](202000002.md) and their [Connection](60006.md) to [Human](40000001.md)-[Existence](60064.md).
