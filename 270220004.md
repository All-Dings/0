## Definition

A Curve is a named bend or connected combination of bends in a Race-Track.
Its Start-Location and End-Location delimit the section in the regular driving direction.
These locations can be represented by separate BCS Earth Point Dings.
The boundaries do not describe the complete geometric course of the Curve.
