# Physical-Altitude

I am a [Physics-Length](10000156.md).

I represent signed vertical Position relative to an explicit Reference Surface. Unknown vertical References remain unknown.

My canonical measurement unit is [Unit-Metre](10000154.md). A negative value
is valid. A GPS observation alone does not establish sea level or an ellipsoid
as its vertical reference.
